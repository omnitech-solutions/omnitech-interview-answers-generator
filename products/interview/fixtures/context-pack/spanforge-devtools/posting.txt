About Spanforge

Spanforge makes slow pipelines fast and opaque pipelines legible. Engineering teams plug our remote cache into the build tool they already use, trace every step of every pipeline, and find out why last night's build took twice as long as the one before it. We were founded in Toronto in 2019. Today about 1,900 organisations run roughly 800,000 builds a day through Spanforge, and we are a Series B company of 165 people.

What we ship

- Spanforge Cache: a content-addressed remote cache and remote build execution service for Bazel, Gradle and Buck2.
- Spanforge Trace: distributed tracing for pipelines, built on OpenTelemetry.
- forge: the command-line tool developers run on their laptops and in pipelines.
- Editor extensions for VS Code and the JetBrains IDEs.
- Spanforge Enterprise: the same product, self-hosted by the customer, including sites with no outbound network.
