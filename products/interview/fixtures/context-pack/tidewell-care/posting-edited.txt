Principal Engineer, Scheduling Core
Tidewell Care · Dublin (hybrid)

About Tidewell Care

Tidewell Care runs appointment scheduling for 1,900 clinics across Ireland and the UK. We began in 2016 as a reminder service for physiotherapy practices and grew into the platform clinics use to take bookings, run waitlists, remind patients and plan clinician rotas. Patients book about 11 million appointments a year through Tidewell, and we send 26 million reminders. We connect to 14 clinic record systems. We are a Series C company of 260 people.

The team

Scheduling Core owns the booking engine, slot availability, the waitlist and the rules that decide who is offered a freed slot. Our sister team, Integrations, owns the connections to clinic record systems. You will join a team of 8 engineers and work beside a product manager and a designer. This is a principal individual-contributor role with no direct reports.

What you will do

- Set technical direction for the booking engine and the waitlist.
- Keep bookings correct under load: a slot is never given to two patients.
- Lead the design of waitlist backfill, so a cancelled slot reaches the next eligible patient quickly and fairly.
- Work with Integrations on HL7 and FHIR interfaces to clinic record systems.
- Finish moving booking writes from our older Ruby application into Go services.
- Define service level objectives for booking and hold the team to them.
- Lead design reviews and keep decisions written down.
- Mentor senior and staff engineers across two teams.
- Share on-call for Scheduling Core.

Our stack

All services are written in Go and run on Kubernetes in AWS. PostgreSQL 15 for data, Kafka for events, Redis for slot holds, Temporal for reminder workflows, OR-Tools for rotas, React and TypeScript on the front end, Terraform and GitHub Actions for delivery, OpenTelemetry and Grafana for observability.

How we work

Patients wait less: every decision is judged by time to care. Clinics trust us with their day, so we do not break it. We write things down. We fix the cause, not the symptom. We are kind and direct.
