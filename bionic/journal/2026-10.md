## [2026-10-03 13:00] decision | Active Session private data and locality ADR accepted after three review rounds

ADR-0012 binds Active Session data to the owner under forced row security, makes device-only a profile-declared locality enforced at resolution and before dispatch, and fixes retention, purge and untrusted-input rules; it amends ADR-0011's end rule.
Review ran as three rounds of five Claude review sub-agents, one deep-architecture review after the split round 1, and one targeted correctness re-check; the multi-model Claude, Gemini and GPT council was not run, because it sends ADR text to external providers and spends money, which PB-0002 does not authorise. Round 3 still ended with one Correctness REQUEST_CHANGES (five interaction defects); I fixed them in place and had that reviewer re-check once, which returned APPROVE_WITH_NITS, rather than convening a fourth round.
The debates that shaped it: a per-row private job marker instead of a table-wide guard on ai.agent_jobs; a trigger, not column privileges, to bound the cross-tenant claim; the on-device relay as a worker-composed device profile or a refusal; credential expiry pausing rather than ending a session; and purge reaching job events, payloads and relay rows.
Friction: the planners' first draft claimed ai.agent_jobs.session_id was unused and that column privileges could limit a setting-based claim; both were false against the code (it is the agent runtime resume id, and there is one application role), found only in round 1. Surprise: the assistant relay table keeps call input outside every table a purge would name.
Deferred: table shapes and migration numbering, numeric limits, the bounded purge wait, removed-member sessions, credential delivery to the companion, and whether the terminal gateway learns the owner.
